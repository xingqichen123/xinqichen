This is a self introduction from a resin sand factory in China

Our factory has been engaged in the production of resin sand for nearly 20 years, and the supply of resin sand raw materials for 30 years. Our products are mainly exported overseas and exported to the United States, Germany, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Taiwan. Reliable quality, highly praised by users.
We have two types of raw materials, melamine and urea, with a complete range of resin sand mesh sizes and abundant inventory of raw materials. We can provide high-performance resin sand blasting products to help you reduce costs and increase efficiency while achieving green production. The process is stable and the effect is precise, especially suitable for industries with strict requirements for surface quality. Welcome to request samples to experience the actual effect.
